1. Rule-Based Heuristic Scanner

Up to 60 Pts

High-speed deterministic pattern matcher evaluating emails against 5 distinct threat dimensions:

  • Urgency & Psychological Pressure: Detects manufactured deadlines (e.g., "account suspended within 24 hours", "immediate action mandatory").
  • Suspicious Links & Domain Spoofing: Flags bare IP URLs, lookalike typosquatting domains (e.g., paypa1.com), and abnormal top-level domains (.xyz, .top).
  • Credential Harvesting Vectors: Identifies explicit calls to re-authenticate, update master passwords, or disclose 2FA tokens.
  • Financial & Wire Fraud Cues: Triggers on unverified invoices, cryptocurrency wallet addresses, and sudden bank account change requests.
  • Header & Greeting Anomalies: Highlights mismatched envelope domains and generic impersonations ("Dear Customer", "Undisclosed Recipient").

2. Neural LLM Intelligence (Ollama)

Up to 40 Pts

Leverages local Large Language Models (such as Llama 3, Mistral, and Phi 3) executed entirely on-premise without cloud data leaks:

  • Contextual Semantic Understanding: Understands nuanced interpersonal tone, professional spear-phishing contexts, and pretexting scenarios.
  • Evidence Extraction: Extracts and isolates the most deceptive sentences directly from the email body.
  • Confidence Scoring: Calculates an uncertainty metric based on language consistency and coherence.
  • 100% Offline Privacy: Emails are never dispatched to third-party APIs. All inference occurs in local GPU/CPU memory via Ollama.

Academic Scope & Limitations

Read Carefully

• Academic Capstone Project: PhishDetect AI is engineered as a 3rd-year university computer science capstone evaluation system. It is intended for educational, research, and proof-of-concept cybersecurity demonstrations.

• No Guarantee of Total Accuracy: While the dual-layer model yields strong heuristic accuracy, modern zero-day attacks and adversarial evasion techniques can circumvent static rules or neural language detectors.

• Advisory Tool Only: This application must never be deployed as the sole security boundary for enterprise email gateways. Always employ multi-layered email defenses (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, sandboxing) and human security awareness.

• Local Hardware Dependence: Response times of the AI evaluation step depend directly on host machine specifications (CPU/GPU acceleration and available RAM).
